# Privacy Policy – One Thumb Army: One Hand Shooter

**Effective Date: October 2, 2026**

Phoenix Play Zone ("we", "our", or "us") provides the mobile game **One Thumb Army: One Hand Shooter** ("the Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the Game.

## Google Play Games Services

The Game may use Google Play Games Services for features such as player authentication and other Play Games functionality.

The Game does not operate its own username/password account system. Information associated with your Google account or Google Play Games profile is handled by Google in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements.

Google's advertising services may collect or process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from IP address
- Device and advertising identifiers
- App and advertisement interactions
- Diagnostic and performance information
- Information used for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention

The information collected and processed by Google is subject to Google's privacy policies and applicable settings and consent requirements.

## Game Data

Game settings and certain gameplay progress may be stored locally on your device.

If Google Play Games features are enabled, Google may process information necessary to provide those features.

We currently do not operate a separate database containing player usernames and passwords.

## Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party services provided by Google, including:

- Google Play Games Services
- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
- Google Play services

These services may collect and process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

If the Game is made available to children in applicable regions, we will follow relevant Google Play Families policies, advertising requirements, and applicable privacy laws.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled directly by the Game.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is protected according to the security practices and policies of those service providers.

## Your Privacy Choices

You may manage certain privacy, advertising, and Google Play Games settings through your Android device and Google account.

You may also manage or delete certain Google Play Games information through Google's Play Games settings.
